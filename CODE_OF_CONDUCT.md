# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and security-conscious project
community. Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate
disclosure of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Use synthetic data in all public collaboration.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and security concerns directly.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about safety, scope, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Publishing personal information, secrets, vulnerabilities, or exploit details
  without authorization.
- Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, or sustained
  disruption.
- Misrepresenting unimplemented behavior or weakening documented privacy
  protections.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@securedact.com`. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. This
role address must be replaced if it is not actively monitored before public
community participation begins.
